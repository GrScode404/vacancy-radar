About the job
instinctools is a software development company that provides custom software solutions for businesses of all sizes. Our team works closely with clients to understand their specific needs and provide personalized solutions that meet their business requirements.
instinctools is looking for a Senior Python Developer (LLM Agents & Data) for one of our clients.

Locations: Europe, Kazakhstan, Georgia, Armenia

Work mode: Fully remote, full-time

Time zone: CET

Duration: Long-term cooperation, extended in 4-month periods based on the client’s confirmation

Client: A global mobility and urban services platform that offers ride-hailing, delivery and other everyday services to users in dozens of countries.

The Project

We are looking for a developer who understands what users need, builds it with AI and makes sure people actually use it.

You Will Join The Client’s BI Analytics Team, Which Builds Internal Analytics Products Powered By LLM Agents For Company Employees. There Are Two Products To Start With

An AI agent that answers natural-language questions about data (Python, LLM APIs and MCP, data from BigQuery, deployed on GCP)
A service that assembles management presentations from prepared data, with LLM-generated text insights (web service with a Python and Postgres backend and a React frontend, data from BigQuery)

The role is hands-on: you write code every day and ship working services that colleagues across the company use.

The core of the work is backend, integrations and infrastructure, with frontend as a smaller part. The team builds on top of existing LLM APIs and does not train models.

Tech stack: Python, LLM APIs and MCP, BigQuery, GCP, Postgres, React (frontend), Docker, CI/CD, Google Workspace and Slack integrations, AI tools

What You Will Get Hands-on With

Talking to the colleagues who use the products, deciding together what to build next and tracking how the products are used
Building LLM agents that answer natural-language questions about company data, using Python, LLM APIs and MCP
Giving agents tools to work with BigQuery, Google Workspace and Slack
Developing a web service that turns prepared data into management presentations with LLM-generated text insights (Python and Postgres backend, React frontend)
Designing evals and monitoring that show how good the answers of LLM components are
Setting up user access (OAuth, IAM) and deploying services on GCP with Docker and CI/CD
Working with AI coding agents such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex as part of your daily workflow

Responsibilities And Tasks

Work with the employees who use the products to understand what they need, and take features from idea and prototype to production
Connect LLM agents to data and internal systems: agent tools and integrations with BigQuery, Google Workspace and Slack
Set up user access and authorization, deploy and maintain services in the cloud
Build evals and monitoring for LLM components, track how the products are used, fix bugs and iterate on user feedback

Our Expectations Of The Candidate

6+ years of total engineering experience, including 4+ years of commercial backend development in Python
Experience with Docker and CI/CD

Experience We Value Most (you Do Not Need To Tick Every Box, But The More Of These You Can Show From Your Own Work, The Better)

First-hand experience talking to users and deciding what to build
Knowledge of your product’s numbers: how many people use it and what changed for them
An AI feature that people use in production, with quality checks (evals) on its answers
Daily coding with an AI assistant (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) and careful review of what it writes
Python, SQL, a cloud data warehouse and user access management (OAuth, IAM)

English proficiency: B1+

Nice To Have

BigQuery and GCP

React
